# Style examples

## Paris street at blue hour

One full-bleed vertical illustration. A narrow Parisian street recedes toward a softly illuminated church tower. Cream Haussmann façades with mansard roofs, dormer windows, wrought-iron balconies and warm amber windows line both sides. A café terrace and a few small pedestrians sit in the middle ground. In the foreground, flowering branches and dense garden foliage frame the street from both upper corners; window boxes and climbing roses overlap the façades. The distant rooftops soften into a lavender-blue evening sky. Monet-inspired broken colour appears in the foliage, flowers, reflected light and sky, while architectural details remain crisp and readable. Strong foreground, middle-ground and background separation, many natural overlaps, rich detail without clutter. No text, no watermark, no collage, no panels, no frame.

## Paris rooftops with Eiffel Tower

One full-bleed vertical illustration from an elevated Paris viewpoint. The Eiffel Tower rises in the middle distance above a dense field of zinc mansard roofs, chimneys and dormer windows. A cream stone apartment building with wrought-iron balconies anchors the left foreground; lush branches and white and coral flowers frame the upper edge and lower corners. Layered rooftops create depth and lead toward the landmark. The sky is luminous powder blue with soft cloud strokes and tiny warm highlights. Delicate architectural linework contrasts with impressionist foliage and broken-colour flowers. Romantic, meticulously detailed, coherent Parisian architecture. No text, no watermark, no collage, no panels, no frame.
